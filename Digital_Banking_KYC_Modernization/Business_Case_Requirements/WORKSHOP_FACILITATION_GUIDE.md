# Requirements Elicitation Workshop Facilitation Guide
**Project:** Digital Banking KYC & Identity Verification Modernization  
**Author / Facilitator:** Adenike Faluwoye, Senior Business Systems Analyst

**Target Audience:** Project Steering Committee & Workshop Attendees

## 1. Executive Summary & Objective
The purpose of this 2-hour interactive session is to analyze the legacy 3-day manual customer onboarding workflow, establish functional requirements for an automated, real-time identity verification pipeline, and gain cross-functional alignment across Compliance, IT, and Operations.

## 2. Session Logistics
* **Date & Time:** October 2026 | 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM CST
* **Format:** Virtual (Microsoft Teams) / Whiteboarding (Miro / Draw.io)
* **Facilitator:** Adenike Faluwoye (Senior Business Systems Analyst)

## 3. Workshop Attendees & Roles
* **Product Owner:** Business Vision & Scope Prioritization
* **Lead BSA (Adenike Faluwoye):** Workshop Facilitation, Process Discovery, & Requirement Capture
* **Project Manager:** Schedule, Dependencies, & Scope Control
* **Compliance & Anti-Fraud Lead:** Regulatory Validation (FINTRAC / OSFI) & Sanction Screening Rules
* **Branch Operations Lead:** As-Is Operational Pains & Exception Handling
* **Tech / Dev Lead:** API Integration Feasibility & Architectural Constraints
* **QA Lead:** Edge Cases, Data Validation, & Acceptance Criteria Rules

## 5. Ground Rules for Participants
1. **Focus on the "What" and "Why" First:** Define business logic before debating technical implementation details.
2. **Compliance is Non-Negotiable:** Regulatory requirements (FINTRAC) take priority over UX speed.
3. **Capture Every Edge Case:** If a process path happens 1% of the time, it must still be documented.
