## 1. RACI Definitions

* **R - Responsible:** The role that performs the activity to achieve the deliverable.
* **A - Accountable:** The sole decision-maker who holds final ownership and approval.
* **C - Consulted:** Subject matter experts who provide crucial input and feedback.
* **I - Informed:** Stakeholders kept updated on progress and outcomes.

> **Note on Compliance (*):** Compliance & Anti-Fraud holds an Accountable (**A**) role specifically for regulatory sign-off on the To-Be Workflow Architecture.

## 3. Key Observations & Operational Alignment

* **Clear Single Ownership (Accountability):** The Product Owner holds primary accountability (**A**) across the project lifecycle, ensuring unified business direction.
* **BSA Delivery Focus:** As Lead BSA, your primary responsibilities (**R**) cover process mapping, workflow architecture, the BRD, API mapping, and operational training readiness.
* **Engineering & Quality Execution:** Tech/Dev Lead is explicitly Responsible (**R**) for API mapping implementation, while QA Lead and Branch Operations share Responsible (**R**) ownership during UAT execution.
